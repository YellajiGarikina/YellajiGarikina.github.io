**Hacker by trade, defender by purpose.**

I've spent the last five years breaking into web applications, APIs, and mobile apps for a living — finding the gaps before someone with worse intentions does. The attack surface never stays still for long, and that's the part I actually enjoy: there's always something new worth poking at.

This blog documents lab walkthroughs, testing methodology, and recurring patterns observed across security testing work.

It also doubles as a running record of that growth — real exploitation work, documented as it happens.
